About SATLab UET

SATLab is a research group focused on Propositional Satisfiability (SAT), MaxSAT, Pseudo-Boolean encodings, and exact decision algorithms for hard combinatorial optimization problems at VNU University of Engineering and Technology (UET-VNU).

Institutional Affiliation

SATLab is affiliated with the Faculty of Information Technology, VNU University of Engineering and Technology (UET-VNU), Hanoi, Vietnam.

Open Science & Reproducibility Principles

We adhere to strict open science standards: publications are accompanied by open-source code repositories, verified benchmarks, and reproducible solver configurations whenever licensing permits.
