About SATLab UET

SATLab UET is a specialized research group investigating Propositional Satisfiability (SAT), MaxSAT, Pseudo-Boolean encodings, automated reasoning, and exact decision solvers for hard combinatorial optimization problems at VNU University of Engineering and Technology (UET-VNU).

History & Foundation

The predecessor of SATLab is the Laboratory of Embedded Systems (PTN Hệ thống nhúng - PTN HTN), officially established on November 15, 2010 (Decision No. 406/QĐ-TCCB&CTSV) at UET-VNU.

The foundational origins trace back to October 11, 2005, under the VNU-approved project for the Smart Integrated Systems Laboratory (SIS). Over 15 years of academic evolution, research led by Dr. To Van Khanh expanded from embedded software verification and SMT test generation (VNU project QG.16.32) into a dedicated research group — SATLab — advancing optimal SAT encodings and exact combinatorial solvers.
