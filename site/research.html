Core Research Domains

SATLab investigates how propositional satisfiability (SAT), MaxSAT, and Pseudo-Boolean (PB) encodings can be formulated to solve hard combinatorial optimization problems with provable correctness.

THEME 01

Encodings & Solver Architecture

We design compact, unit-propagation-effective SAT encodings for cardinality constraints (AMO, AMK, ALK) and Pseudo-Boolean constraints. Key breakthroughs include the New Sequential Counter (NSC) register and the SCLib solver ecosystem.

THEME 02

Scheduling & Line Balancing

Industrial optimization problems including Assembly Line Balancing (SALBP, UALBP), Power Peak Minimization, Job-Shop Scheduling (JSSP/FJSSP), and Railway Train Rescheduling using MaxSAT-DDD.

View Publications ↗ View Active Projects ↗
THEME 03

2D Packing & Cutting Optimization

Exact SAT models for 2D Strip Packing Problems, 2D Bin Packing, and Cutting Stock Problems using integer layout modeling and non-overlapping spatial decomposition.

View Publications ↗
THEME 04

Graph Labeling & Frequency Assignment

Exact decision algorithms for Antibandwidth, Cyclic Antibandwidth, Bandwidth Multicoloring, Radio-k Labeling, and Multi-Objective Frequency Assignment (MO-FAP) in wireless networks.
