Core Research Domains

Our research spans fundamental theoretical encodings to industrial combinatorial optimization applications.

THEORETICAL CORE

1. SAT & Pseudo-Boolean Encodings

Developing New Sequential Counter (NSC) variants, Cardinality encodings (AMO/AMK/ALK), and Pseudo-Boolean translations for CNF solvers.

INDUSTRIAL APPS

2. Assembly Line Balancing & Scheduling

Exact decision algorithms for Power Peak Minimization in SALB/UALBP, Job-Shop Scheduling, and Train Rescheduling via MaxSAT-DDD.

GEOMETRIC OPTIMIZATION

3. 2D Strip & Bin Packing

Formulating non-overlapping spatial constraints and rotation-free models for 2D Strip Packing and Cutting Stock Problems.

GRAPH ALGORITHMS

4. Graph Labeling & Frequency Assignment

Solving Antibandwidth, Cyclic Antibandwidth, Radio-k Labeling, Bandwidth Multicoloring, and Mobile Frequency Allocation via SAT.
