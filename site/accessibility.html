Accessibility Statement

SATLab UET is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. We continuously improve the user experience for everyone and apply the relevant accessibility standards targeting WCAG 2.2 Level AA conformance.

Measures Taken

  • Semantic HTML5 landmarks (<header>, <nav>, <main>, <footer>).
  • Full keyboard accessibility with visible focus indicators.
  • Color contrast ratios meeting or exceeding 4.5:1 for normal text.
  • Screen reader compatible ARIA attributes and image alt texts.

Feedback & Contact

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of the SATLab website. If you encounter accessibility barriers, please contact us at khanhtv@vnu.edu.vn.
