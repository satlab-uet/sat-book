Accessibility Statement

SATLab UET is committed to digital accessibility for people with disabilities. We target WCAG 2.2 Level AA conformance across all pages.

Measures Taken

  • Semantic HTML5 landmarks (<header>, <nav>, <main>, <footer>).
  • Full keyboard accessibility with visible focus indicators.
  • Color contrast ratios meeting WCAG 2.2 AA standards.
  • Accessible ARIA labels and descriptive alt text.

Feedback & Contact

Please report accessibility barriers to khanhtv@vnu.edu.vn.
