Research Projects

Active, completed, and exploratory research initiatives developing solvers, proof systems, and industrial optimization applications.

Active Projects

ACTIVE 2024 – Present

SCLib: Shared Counter Library & Solver Ecosystem

A high-performance C++ library implementing adaptive sequential counter encodings (NSC) for Pseudo-Boolean and Cardinality constraints.

Leads: Truong Xuan Hieu, To Van Khanh
ACTIVE 2025 – Present

MaxSAT-DDD: Railway Train Rescheduling System

Dynamic Decoupled Domain (DDD) reasoning combined with MaxSAT for real-time train rescheduling under infrastructure disruptions.

Leads: Kieu Van Tuyen, To Van Khanh

Completed Projects

COMPLETED 2023 – 2025

SAT Encodings for 2D Strip Packing

Optimal spatial decomposition and non-overlapping constraint models for 2D Strip Packing Problems, published in ICAART 2025.

Outcome: Paper accepted, benchmark dataset released.
